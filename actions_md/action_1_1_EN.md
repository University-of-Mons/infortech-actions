# Support for scientific publications: Scientific collaboration award

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Bonus for publishing a scientific article** in an internationally renowned journal or in the proceedings of a conference ranked A or A*.
This action aims to *promote scientific collaboration* between several teams (intra- or inter-university) around one of INFORTECH's research themes.

## Proposed funding

- 300€ if only one INFORTECH member department is involved;
- 600€ if co-authors from several INFORTECH member departments are involved (intra-university collaboration).

## Deadline

This bonus must be requested *at most 6 months* after the article's publication date.

## Criteria

- The applicant (or co-author) may not benefit from more than one request of this type per year.
- The support must concern an article published in a high-quality international peer-reviewed scientific journal, or in a scientific conference ranked A or A* according to CORE2023 at [http://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/](http://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/).
- The article and proof of its publication must be attached to the funding request.
- The article's subject must fall within INFORTECH's research themes and must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository (with access to the article's PDF), referencing INFORTECH as the Research Institute.
- The article must have either at least two co-authors belonging to different INFORTECH member departments (intra-university collaboration), or at least one co-author from an INFORTECH member department and at least one co-author from a team outside UMONS (inter-university collaboration).
- One of the article's co-authors must present it at an INFORTECH seminar.

## Decision process

The BDIR handles the request.
