# Virtual presentation at an international conference

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Support for the registration fees for a scientific presentation at an international conference** (or colloquium / conference / seminar / summer school / …) **in virtual mode**.
This action reflects a wish by the University and Europe to limit travel, which could favour the continuation of hybrid conferences. The FNRS only covers travel costs, whereas a virtual conference involves no travel. There are also no accommodation costs for a virtual conference.

## Proposed funding

Registration fees capped at 400€ per request.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **before the conference start date**.

## Criteria

- The support must concern the presentation of a peer-reviewed article accepted for publication. This article must be attached to the funding request. The article's subject must fall within INFORTECH's research themes. The article must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository, referencing INFORTECH as the affiliated Institute.
- The applicant must be the author presenting the article. Other co-authors may not benefit from this support.
- The applicant must present their article at an INFORTECH seminar.

## Decision process

The BDIR handles the request.
