# Presentation at a scientific conference abroad

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Support for the costs of presenting a scientific communication at a conference abroad** (or colloquium / conference / seminar / summer school / …).

## Proposed funding

Support for travel, accommodation, or registration costs. Capped at **600€** per request within Europe, and **1200€** per request outside Europe.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at least 1 month before the conference date**.

Warning! The deadlines imposed by the FNRS are much stricter!

## Criteria

- The support must concern a peer-reviewed article accepted for publication. This article must be attached to the funding request. The article's subject must fall within INFORTECH's research themes. The article must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository, referencing INFORTECH as the institute.
- The applicant must be the author presenting the article. Other co-authors may not benefit from this support.
- This support is **only available as a complement to a CRECH scientific mission abroad request**.
- If eligible, an FNRS request should have been submitted by the applicant.
- The applicant must present their article at an INFORTECH seminar.

## Decision process

The BDIR handles the request.
