# Bridging grant

## Objective

**FRIA/FRESH bridging grants (3 months)** intended to cover the period from 1 October to 31 December that separates the grant application from its award.

## Proposed funding

3 months of tax-exempt grant. If co-funded by several institutes, funding pro rata to the participation in INFORTECH.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest at the time of submission of the FRIA / FRESH application**.

## Criteria

- The candidate's file must fall within INFORTECH's research themes. The proposed research project must be of good quality, and the candidate's CV must be of good quality.
- The doctoral student must belong to an INFORTECH member department.
- The same department may not submit more than one request of this type per year.
- The candidate's thesis must be (at least partially) affiliated with INFORTECH. If partially funded by several institutes, affiliation pro rata to the participation in INFORTECH.
- The candidate must have already applied, during the same year, for a FRIA / FRESH grant, in compliance with the respective rules of those calls.
- Must meet the conditions to benefit from a tax-exempt grant.
- Must never have previously benefited from this action.
- If the FRIA grant is obtained, the funding of the bridging grant will be recovered.

## Decision process

Decision by the CDIR, based on the quality of the CV and the project proposal. The CDIR reserves the right not to award the grant.
