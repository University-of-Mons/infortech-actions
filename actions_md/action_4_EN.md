# Research initiation grant

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Student internship aimed at providing help (from students at the end of their bachelor's cycle) to doctoral students in the context of their research work.** The internship will last 4 weeks (continuous or non-continuous) during the summer corresponding to the transition from bachelor's to master's degree.

## Proposed funding

- maximum 4 weeks of student job

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **simultaneously with the request to the CRECH**.

## Criteria

- The doctoral student submits the request. At most one request per doctoral student per year, and the doctoral student must belong to an INFORTECH member department.
- At the end of the internship, the student must give a presentation of their work at an INFORTECH seminar.
- Same eligibility criteria as for the CRECH. The doctoral student must have submitted the same eligible request to the CRECH during the same year. The INFORTECH grant thus makes it possible to rescue grant requests that could not be funded.
- Research initiation work funded by the grant may not correspond to the mandatory internships of the student's curriculum.
- The topic of the research initiation internship must be related to one of INFORTECH's themes.

## Decision process

By the CDIR, selection based on the ranking established by the faculties for eligible research initiation grants not funded by the CRECH.
