# Partially funded doctoral grant

## Objective

**Partially funded doctoral grant enabling the rescue of a good candidate who submitted an eligible request as part of the CRECH call.**

## Proposed funding

Co-funding of 50% of a doctoral grant, for a duration of at most 4 years (for grants with France, typically only 3 years).

## Deadline

The request must be submitted at the same time as the submission to the CRECH.

## Criteria

- An eligible request must have been submitted to the CRECH.
- The thesis topic must fall within one of INFORTECH's themes. The candidate's thesis must be affiliated at least partially with INFORTECH. If partially funded by several institutes, affiliation pro rata to INFORTECH's financial participation.
- Graduate student, or expected to graduate within the current year, wishing to carry out a doctoral thesis in an INFORTECH member department.
- The candidate may or may not meet the conditions for tax exemption. If the candidate does not meet the conditions for tax exemption, INFORTECH funds only the equivalent of the amount of a tax-exempt grant, the remainder being borne by the requesting department.
- The candidate must have obtained bachelor's AND master's cycle grades above 70% or be in the top 25% of their class (official document as proof).

## Decision process

The number of grants awarded will depend on the budget remaining available at the time of the CDIR's decision on the requests received. The decision is based on the CRECH ranking, as well as the ranking submitted by the faculties and the candidate's file. The CDIR reserves the right not to award the grant. If there are several eligible requests for this type of action, the CDIR will take into account requests approved in previous years, in order to avoid the same department always benefiting from this type of funding.
