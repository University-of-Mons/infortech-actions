# PhD thesis completion grant

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

*Grant intended to complete a thesis carried out by a doctoral student at UMONS who has benefited from at least 3 years (but less than 4 years) of doctoral funding.*

A few examples of typical cases: (1) a co-supervised thesis with France lasts 3 years, and could be extended to reach 4 years; (2) a doctoral student enrolled in a thesis reaches the end of a 3-year mandate funded by a European or FEDER-FSE project.

## Proposed funding

- Up to **6 months per request** for a tax-exempt PhD scholarship.
- Up to **3 months per request** for an employment contract.

The department must find co-funding (e.g. from the department itself, the CRECH, another institute, a research project) for at least the same duration as that granted by INFORTECH.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest two months before the start of the grant**.

## Criteria

- A recent report from the thesis follow-up committee with a favourable opinion on the continuation and completion of the thesis in the coming year.
- The thesis must be (at least partially) affiliated with INFORTECH. If partially funded by several institutes, the thesis affiliation will be pro rata to the participation in each institute.
- If eligible, the doctoral student must have first requested a thesis completion grant from the CRECH (up to 6 months; the CRECH grant is only accessible for European projects other than FEDER-FSE, and in the context of extending a thesis with France outside a European project, for example, the CRECH cannot intervene). The INFORTECH request makes it possible to supplement this duration with 3 additional months.

## Decision process

By the CDIR, which reserves the right not to award the grant, to limit the number of co-funded months based on the available budget, or to limit the number of requests per thesis supervisor or per department.
