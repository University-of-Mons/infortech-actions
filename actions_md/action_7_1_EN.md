# Research stay abroad (for doctoral student or post-doc)

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Partial support for the funding of INFORTECH doctoral students or post-docs wishing to carry out a long-term research stay (minimum one month) in a research team abroad.** This support complements requests made to the FNRS and the CRECH or other external funding sources.

## Proposed funding

A flat rate of 600€ per month of stay for a maximum of 3 months.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest two months before the stay**.

## Criteria

- For stays of at least 1 month. The stay's duration *may* exceed 3 months, and this mobility support can then be used to co-fund beyond the 3 months funded by the CRECH (for example, co-funding of the first 3 months by CRECH and the last 3 months by INFORTECH). The minimum of 1 month cannot be split.
- The research topic of the stay must fall within one of INFORTECH's research themes.
- After their stay, the researcher must give a presentation of their work at an INFORTECH seminar.
- The stay must aim at a scientific collaboration with the objective of joint scientific publications. The article must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository, referencing INFORTECH as the institute.
- For doctors and doctoral students funded by the FNRS/FRIA/FRESH, the applicant must have first submitted an FNRS OUT scientific stay grant request.
- The applicant must have first submitted an eligible CRECH request (Franeau Mobility Fund or FWB Travel Grant).
- The submitted file must justify the purpose of the stay and the amount requested from INFORTECH and other funding sources.

## Decision process

By the BDIR, which reserves the right not to grant the request, or to limit the number of requests granted per department.
