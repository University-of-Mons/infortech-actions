# Support for accommodation and travel costs for incoming post-doc

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

Hosting of post-docs for a duration of at least two weeks. **Support for accommodation and travel costs.**

## Proposed funding

At most 1,200€ per month of stay (capped at 3 months), and only to cover accommodation and travel costs based on supporting documents.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest two months before the stay**.

## Criteria

- Not cumulable with the action [Mobility IN: tax-exempt postdoctoral grant](action_7_3_EN.md).
- For stays of at least two weeks.
- The stay must aim at a scientific collaboration within one of INFORTECH's research themes, with the objective of joint scientific publications.
- Resulting scientific publications must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository, referencing INFORTECH.
- If eligible, the applicant must have first submitted a request to the FNRS (3 months in advance).
- During their stay, the post-doc commits to giving a research talk at an INFORTECH seminar.
- The same department may not submit more than one request of this type per year.

## Decision process

By the CDIR.
