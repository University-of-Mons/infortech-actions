# Mobility IN/OUT: Mobility IN: tax-exempt postdoctoral grant

## Objective

**Hosting of post-docs in the form of a tax-exempt postdoctoral grant.**

## Proposed funding

At most **6 months** of tax-exempt postdoctoral grant.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest two months before the stay**.

## Criteria

- Not cumulable with the action [Mobility IN: support for accommodation and travel costs (for post-doc)](action_7_2_EN.md).
- The post-doc must meet the conditions for tax exemption.
- For stays of at least 1 month. The stay's duration *may* exceed 3 months, but funding will be limited to 3 months.
- The research topic of the stay must fall within one of INFORTECH's research themes.
- The stay must aim at a scientific collaboration with the objective of joint scientific publications. The article must be recorded in the UMONS Institutional Repository, referencing INFORTECH as the institute.
- If eligible, the applicant must have first submitted a request to the FNRS (3 months in advance).
- The post-doc commits to giving a research talk, during their stay, as part of the INFORTECH seminars.
- The same department may not submit more than one request of this type per year.

## Decision process

By the CDIR. Based on the available budget, the CDIR reserves the right not to award the grant, or to limit the duration of the grant awarded.
