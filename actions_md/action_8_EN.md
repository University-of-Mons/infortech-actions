# Short-term visits of foreign researchers

[[Back to overview of all actions]](../README.md)

## Objective

**Short-term visits of foreign researchers** (also applicable to doctoral researchers) to collaborate with one or more INFORTECH departments.

## Proposed funding

Coverage of accommodation and travel, based on supporting documents, up to a maximum of 120€ per day and capped at 600€ per request.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest one month before the visit**.

## Criteria

- The visit must last **at least 5 consecutive working days**.
- At the end of the visit, the applicant sends a report summarising the research work carried out to the INFORTECH president and/or gives a presentation at an INFORTECH seminar.
- The visit must aim at a collaboration with INFORTECH members within one of its research themes.
- If eligible, a request must have been submitted for the CRECH "Open Research" action (stays of scientific experts to provide scientific training to UMONS doctoral students, or events dedicated to all doctoral schools).
- The same department may not submit more than one request of this type per year.

## Decision process

By the BDIR.
