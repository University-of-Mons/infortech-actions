# Support for organising conferences

## Objective

**Partial support for the costs of organising a scientific colloquium** (or seminar / conference / summer school / congress / ...).

## Proposed funding

Maximum **2500€ per request** based on supporting documents, and only as a complement to other funding requests submitted for the same colloquium.

## Deadline

Requests must be submitted **at the latest two months before the start of the colloquium**.

## Criteria

- The colloquium's subject must fall within INFORTECH's themes.
- The applicant must be an INFORTECH member and co-organiser of the colloquium.
- At the end of the colloquium, the applicant sends a report summarising the colloquium to the INFORTECH president.
- The applicant must have submitted a CRECH colloquium organisation support request, if eligible.
- The applicant must have submitted a request to the FNRS and/or the Walloon Region or other research funds, if eligible.
- The applicant must have additionally submitted a request to the doctoral school, if eligible.
- A detailed budget estimate (income and expenses) of the colloquium must be provided, specifying which part(s) the support requested from INFORTECH covers.
- The applicant may not submit more than one request of this type per calendar year.

## Decision process

By the BDIR if the funding request does not exceed 600€; by the CDIR otherwise.
